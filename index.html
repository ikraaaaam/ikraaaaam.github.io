Research

~/ikraaaaam/research

Current work at CUET — and the question behind the planned PhD.

Manuscript submitted · IEEE ECCE 2027

Decoding cognitive workload from EEG & ECG — on people the model has never seen

A 27-participant study combining 24-channel EEG, chest-strap ECG and NASA-TLX workload ratings. The central problem: models that work on familiar participants collapse on new ones, because the shift from rest to task is far larger than the workload signal itself.

Subtracting each person's resting baseline pushed unseen-person decoding to chance — the rest-to-task shift was 4.2× larger than the load effect. Standardising each session without labels recovered most of the signal. Two journal manuscripts are in preparation, and the planned PhD extends this: which periodic and aperiodic working-memory EEG markers stay reliable across people, sessions and cleaning pipelines, and whether cardiac signals make them more robust.

46.3%Unseen-person decoding accuracy with resting-baseline subtraction — effectively chance
77.8%Same decoding after per-session standardisation without labels
